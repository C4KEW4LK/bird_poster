SD card

This board also reads a pack from its microSD card, where the plates keep their full size — 1200 px on the longest side, against the sizes the flash images above can hold — so every feather survives to the dither. Download a region, copy the file to the root of a FAT32 card as it is named here, and put the card in the slot: the frame uses it over the flash copy on its next page, whichever flash image is on the board. The flash plates stay as the fallback for a frame without a card.

    How

    1. Plug the frame in over USB-C.
    2. Press Install, pick the board's port in the browser's dialog, answer the erase question.
    3. No port listed? Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, and try again.
    4. When it restarts the glass shows a setup page with a QR code: scan it to join the frame's own network and the settings page opens. On a board with every region's plates, the artwork is chosen there.
    5. On the reTerminal, every region holds all three regions and the region is a setting; one region alone gives that region the whole 31 MB, about one and a half times the sprite size, and the region is chosen here instead. The SD card below beats both.
