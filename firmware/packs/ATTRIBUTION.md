# Plate packs - attribution and licensing

Every `.bin` here is a plate pack: the cut-out bird plates of one artwork style
(`au`, `eu` or `us` under `assets/artwork/`), baked for one board's flash or
for the reTerminal E1004's SD card. A pack is a derivative of that artwork and
carries its terms. **All three styles are CC BY-SA 4.0**, so every pack is
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): use and share
it, credit the sources below, and pass the same terms on.

| pack | artwork | sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `*/au.bin` | `assets/artwork/au` | John Gould, *The Birds of Australia* (1840-48), lithographed by Elizabeth Gould and H. C. Richter, from rawpixel's digitally enhanced scans on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0); plus one plate apiece from other public-domain works on Wikimedia Commons for the birds Gould never drew, each credited in the style's `manifest.json`. Cut for this project. |
| `*/eu.bin` | `assets/artwork/eu` | John Gould, *The Birds of Europe* (1832-37), and the von Wright brothers, *Svenska Fåglar*; rawpixel-enhanced scans (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Finnish National Gallery scans (CC0). Cut-outs by Arne Giacomo Munthe-Kaas for [Fugleramme](https://github.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme), CC BY-SA 4.0. |
| `*/us.bin` | `assets/artwork/us` | John James Audubon, *The Birds of America* (1827-38), engraved by Robert Havell Jr., from rawpixel's digitally enhanced scans on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0); plus one plate apiece from other public-domain works on Wikimedia Commons for the birds Audubon never drew, each credited in the style's `manifest.json`. Cut for this project. |

The full attribution for a style - every work, every artist, the licence each
plate carried on Commons - is its `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `manifest.json` under
`assets/artwork/<style>/`. The label boxes in a pack are measured in Gentium
Book Plus (SIL International, OFL 1.1); no font is embedded.

Packs are baked on the workstation that holds the artwork (not part of this
repository); this file travels with them as a release asset.
